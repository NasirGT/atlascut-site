Breaking news and unbelievable true stories, told on animated maps.

AtlasCut makes short map videos for TikTok (@atlascutgeopolitics), YouTube and Instagram. Every video follows the real people involved, with real photos, quotes and headlines.

AtlasCut Uploader

AtlasCut Uploader is an internal tool used only by AtlasCut. It uploads and schedules AtlasCut's own finished videos to AtlasCut's own accounts. It does not offer accounts to the public and does not post on behalf of anyone else.
